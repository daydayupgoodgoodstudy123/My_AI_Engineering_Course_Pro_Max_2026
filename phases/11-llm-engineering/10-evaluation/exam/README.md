# EvalPair

You have 60 minutes to work on a small offline evaluation service. Explain your reasoning as you go. Tests are locked by part; unlock one part at a time and preserve the assertions.

## Pt 1 `test_typed_judge_and_sequence_metric`

Get the existing judge and sequence-metric path working.

## Pt 2 `test_immutable_evaluation_bank`

Persist and reopen complete evaluation runs.

## Pt 3 `test_paired_stratified_bootstrap`

Compare paired prompt-version observations with the declared uncertainty contract.

## Pt 4 `test_completed_evaluation_junit`

Encode one complete local JUnit testcase from its supplied completion. The finite suite, asynchronous publication and source ownership driver are supplied.

## Pt 5 `test_category_paired_coverage_tree`

Build the declared category coverage report while retaining comparison evidence.
